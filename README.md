# Qavex Kalooki Deluxe

Steel drums drift over a Jamaican beach club at sunset. Captain Kaya deals from twin decks with a grin: jokers are wild, and every round demands a tougher contract. Qavex Kalooki Deluxe plays the beloved Jamaican contract rummy across six rounds — two sets of three, then sets plus runs of four, up to triple runs of four. Thirteen cards, four wild jokers, and a rival who always seems to know your hand. Lowest total after six rounds wins, across 8 modes and daily seeded deals.
